Create a Defra account Using Defra services as an organisation

To register your organisation, we need the following information from you.

This can take up to 10 minutes.

Personal contact details

  • your name
  • your phone number

Organisation details

  • company registration number or charity registration number, if applicable
  • registered address
  • business contact details, in case you leave
